About me

I've enjoyed mathematics since I was a kid. At university I studied it, specialising in probability and mathematical statistics, which gave me a strong theoretical and practical foundation for AI/ML.

From there I moved into classical ML: training, evaluating and deploying models. In recent years my focus has shifted to agentic AI systems, and to making them reliable and scalable in production. I've also led small engineering teams hands-on, from requirements to delivery.

Alongside agentic systems, I'm obsessed with voice AI. Voice is the most natural way we communicate, and I believe it's the future of how we'll interact with AI, so on the side I'm building Dentilia.ai, a real-time voice receptionist for dental clinics.

I also love nature, from hiking and backcountry skiing to road cycling, and I competed in judo for 12 years until university, earned a black belt and still train from time to time.
